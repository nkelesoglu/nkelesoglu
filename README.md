# Hi, I'm Nur Keleşoğlu 👋

**Research Assistant | Generative AI | Neuro-Symbolic AI | Machine Learning | Network Traffic | IoT**

I am a Research Assistant at the **Institute of Theoretical and Applied Informatics, Polish Academy of Sciences (IITiS PAN)** in Gliwice, Poland.

My research focuses on developing intelligent and trustworthy AI systems, with particular interest in Large Language Models (LLMs), machine learning, knowledge graphs, and network intelligence.

I am interested in bridging the gap between generative AI and real-world engineering applications, particularly in IoT environments, wireless networks, and cybersecurity.


## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Generative AI & LLMs:** LLM-based data generation, multi-agent systems, and trustworthy AI.
- **Neuro-Symbolic AI:** Knowledge graph-enhanced LLMs, semantic reasoning, and knowledge-grounded generation.
- **Cybersecurity & Network Intelligence:** Intrusion detection, network traffic analysis, and AI-driven security monitoring.
- **IoT & Wireless Networks:** LoRaWAN, ZigBee, LPWAN, and machine learning for wireless systems.
- **Robotics & Human-Robot Interaction (HRI):** LLM-powered robotic systems, natural language-based robot interaction, socially assistive robots, and risk-aware human-robot communication.
- **Explainable AI:** Anomaly detection, risk-aware AI, and interpretable machine learning.
